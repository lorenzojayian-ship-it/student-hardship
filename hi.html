About Student Hardships

Being a student can be challenging. Students face different problems that can affect their studies and daily life.

Common Hardships

Academic Pressure

Students may have many assignments, projects, exams, and deadlines.

Time Management

Balancing school, family, hobbies, and rest can be difficult.

Financial Problems

Some students have difficulty getting school supplies and transportation.

Social Problems

Making friends and dealing with conflicts can sometimes be difficult.

How Can We Help?

  • Ask teachers for help.
  • Make a simple study schedule.
  • Get enough rest.
  • Talk to trusted friends or family.
  • Support classmates who are struggling.
